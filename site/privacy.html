The Utah View

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

The Utah View (“we,” “us”) publishes independent global-politics journalism from Salt Lake City. This policy explains what the app and website collect, why, and your choices. Reading The Utah View is free, and most of it works without giving us any personal information.

Information we collect

  • Email address — only if you choose to create an account or subscribe to our newsletter. We never require an account to read.
  • Password — if you create an email account. Passwords are never stored in readable form; we keep only a salted PBKDF2 hash.
  • Name and sign-in identifier — if you choose to sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, that provider shares your email address, an account identifier, and (if you allow it) your name with us. We use these only to create and label your account. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email; we then receive a private relay address. We also keep a token from Apple so we can revoke Apple’s connection when you delete your account.
  • Error reports — if you use “Report an error” on a story, we receive your message, the page it was about, and your email address only if you enter one, so an editor can follow up.
  • Saved stories — the stories you bookmark. These stay on your device, and are synced to our server only if you are signed in to an account.
  • Notification identifier — if you enable notifications, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns a device token so we can send you a message when a new issue is published. We use it only to deliver notifications.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or financial information. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not run analytics beyond what is needed to deliver notifications.

How we use information

  • To let you sign in and sync your saved stories across devices.
  • To send the newsletter or push notifications you asked for.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app and website.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers strictly to run the service: Cloudflare (hosting, storage, and our database), Google Firebase (push notifications), Google and Apple (only if you choose to sign in with them), and Resend (delivering our emails, including error reports to our editors). They process data on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices and deletion

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email.
  • Turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Delete your account and its data at any time: in the app under More → Delete account, on the web at theutahview.com/delete-account.html, or by emailing theutahview@gmail.com. Deletion removes your account, saved-story data, and newsletter subscription immediately, and revokes Sign in with Apple if you used it.

Children

The Utah View is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email theutahview@gmail.com.
