Delete your account

For readers of The Utah View app and website

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your account (email address, name and password hash),
  • the saved stories synced to your account, and
  • any newsletter subscription for the same email.

Deletion takes effect immediately and can’t be undone. We keep no copy of this data afterward. Bookmarks stored on your own device stay there until you remove them or uninstall the app.

Email & password accounts

Signed in with Google?

Open the app, go to More → Delete account. Or email theutahview@gmail.com from that Google address and we’ll delete everything within 7 days.
