## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
The Utah View is an independent publication of global-politics journalism and analysis, based in Salt Lake City and published monthly. The app brings each issue to your phone in a clean, distraction-free reading experience built for people who want depth, not noise.

WHAT YOU GET
• A new issue every month — reported features, editorials, and analysis on world affairs.
• Organized the way an editor would arrange a front page: a lead story, Editorial & Opinion, Around the World (The Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, and Africa), In This Issue, The Utah Lens, and a Data Brief.
• A focused reader with adjustable text size, light and dark modes, and a reading-progress indicator.

READ YOUR WAY
• Save stories to read later — your bookmarks are available offline.
• Create a free account (optional) to keep your saved stories in sync across devices.
• Share any story as a clean, branded image for social media, or as a link.

STAY CURRENT
• Turn on notifications to know the moment a new issue or story is published.

ABOUT US
The Utah View is editorially independent. We cover global politics with rigor and clarity, from a Salt Lake City point of view. Reading the app is free.

Questions or feedback? Email theutahview@gmail.com or visit theutahview.com.
```
